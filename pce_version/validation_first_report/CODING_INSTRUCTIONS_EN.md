# Independent human coding: first available subsequent report

Read [protocol v1](../research/FIRST_FOLLOWUP_PROTOCOL_V1.md) before coding. Use the case card to open the complete original and the selected first available subsequent report. The archived original excerpt can start or end mid-sentence. Check the surrounding original passage and full raw source before determining the undertaking. The selected report year is an inventory selection; verify the entity, perimeter, period and edition. Report-year gap is not elapsed time. **Do not use any report after the selected report**, even if you know one exists. This exercise has a different observation window from the legacy F5 all-later 76-case kit, so its codes cannot be directly compared with that kit. Do not open its hidden key.

Two independent, language-competent human coders each complete all 183 cases on separate copies of the blank A/B CSV. Country and language remain visible; do not claim country blindness. Do not consult model outcomes or another coder's responses. Save immutable completed rounds before comparison or discussion. Record your `coder_id`, work time, source language, searches and exact original-language quotations with page, section or text locator. Treat the case card's context as a search aid; verify it against the full source.

For each case, work in this order:

1. **Source and entity:** Verify access, readable full context, extraction quality, selected report period, original and subsequent entity/perimeter, and documentary evidence of any name, group or successor link. Use `entity_linkage=yes/no/uncertain` independently. A shared name or AI topic alone is insufficient. Log retrieval attempts and recovery limits. Select `source_adequacy`; inaccessible, unreadable, incomplete extraction/search and ambiguous evidence are distinct from adequate evidence.
2. **Eligibility and original ambition:** Decide whether the original context contains the company's own undertaking to implement, use or scale AI. Record `eligible`, `not_a_promise` or `unresolved` separately from status, with an exact quotation and locator. Extract the promised AI function/object, destination (`study`, `pilot_or_test`, `deployment`, `other`, `unspecified`, `source_unavailable`), initial maturity, scale/scope, specificity basis and deadline wording. A specific commitment names a concrete AI process, function, product or area **and** at least one of: an already achieved pilot/test/deployed stage, a deadline/time frame, quantitative target/result, investment amount, named AI solution/model/platform, or named external partner/vendor/institution. Future intention alone is not an achieved initial stage. A law or market trend is not the company's promise. Keep unspecified distinct from unavailable source.
3. **Dates:** Record original and subsequent reporting-period end dates and publication dates independently, with source locator. Mark each date `known`, `unknown` or `unverified`. Do not invent a date. Record deadline precision; resolve a relative deadline only from an explicit reference date. Classify elapsed/not-yet-elapsed/indeterminate only with a stated reference date and compatible precision. Fulfilment and timeliness are separate. Record a reported event date separately from report/publication dates.
4. **Search selected report:** Search the *whole* available selected report in source language, including product, process, partner, spelling variants and synonyms without an AI keyword. Inspect relevant tables/images and extraction quality. In `search_audit`, write locations searched, terms/variants, OCR/raw checks and negative-search limits. Record `object_linkage=yes/no/uncertain` and `ai_function_linkage=yes/no/uncertain` separately, with rationale. A digital host platform alone does not establish operation of its AI function.
5. **Status only if assessable:** Enter one canonical status only when the original promise and selected report permit an evidence-based judgement. `fulfilled`: same promised AI function achieved at promised stage and scale; a completed/live study or pilot suffices if that was promised; broad AI intentions require at least one named implemented AI process in scope. `partial`: achieved AI function at narrower scale, some promised functions, or live pilot where deployment was promised. `not_fulfilled`: identifiable AI continuation is only development/preparation/test without live pilot, renewed plan, postponement or cancellation. Record cancellation separately. `dropped`: after adequate search of an accessible readable selected report, no identifiable continuation of the AI component is located; this is not abandonment. `not_a_promise`: original context establishes no company AI undertaking; also record `eligibility=not_a_promise`. Require exact original and follow-up quotations/locators for positive evidence and an auditable search for `dropped`. Explain identity, stage, scope and AI-function reasoning.

**Unresolved rule:** If the selected source is unavailable, unreadable or insufficient; the entity link is uncertain; or object/AI-function evidence is ambiguous, record the problem and leave `status` **blank**. Use `eligibility=unresolved` when original eligibility itself is unclear. Never force a best guess from other accessible reports. A missing report is neither `dropped` nor `not_fulfilled`. A host-only continuation with no traced AI component can be `dropped` only after adequate search; a traced AI component still in preparation is `not_fulfilled`. Do not infer AI operation from generic digitalisation or later achievements.

The A and B sheets must remain separate until both are closed. Comparison, disagreement adjudication and any model-key comparison are later steps outside this packet. No human result exists merely because a form or preflight audit exists.
