IKO MOBILE BANKING PKO Bank Polski SA offers advanced technological solutions to its customers, providing them with complete, simple, functional and at the same time safe access to banking services using telephones. The app currently has more than 100 features and offers a wide range of services, including non-banking ones. The number of active IKO applications reached a record high of nearly 7.8 million on the Polish banking market at the end of 2023.
