# First available subsequent report: protocol v1

Date: 15 October 2026. Status: frozen v1 before first-report coding; no completed coding, validation or analysis is implied. Availability inventory: analysis/out/first_followup_source_urls.csv. This version selects the highest documented revision of the same ESEF reporting period when the URL archive contains multiple revisions; edition differences remain subject to source checks. This protocol addresses panel C2/C7; it does not resolve the selection or human-validation objections.

## 1. Target and source selection

The target is the reported status of each eligible AI undertaking in its **first available subsequent annual report**, relative to the original promised object, stage and scope. It is not implementation, on-time delivery, technological success or disclosure quality. The existing 183 retained commitments form a provisional worklist, not a newly validated population. Reassess the 23 unresolved omitted candidates separately; add restored eligible cases transparently, retaining results for the original and corrected populations when outcomes become available.

Select the earliest report year strictly after the commitment year in a frozen, documented availability inventory. Record the inventory cutoff, search coverage, report period, source URL/path, retrieval date and stable file identifier. Label this “first available”, never “first published” unless publication history has independently been established. Do not replace an unreadable or unretrieved selected report with a later readable one in the primary comparison. A newly recovered earlier report requires a versioned inventory correction and recoding, not an outcome-driven substitution. Resolve multiple editions of the same reporting period through a documented content/edition check before coding; retain unresolved source ambiguity as missing.

Confirm the **same reporting entity and perimeter**: legal entity, consolidated group, subsidiary, merger or successor. A group report is usable only when it covers the original undertaking and provides an attributable continuation. A shared name, sector or AI category is insufficient. Record original and follow-up entity/perimeter, the documentary basis of any succession, and changes in coverage. Unresolved entity linkage is an unresolved observation, not a negative outcome. Preserve distinct undertakings and flag repeated episodes across years; do not merge projects merely because firm × category × year matches.

## 2. Timing and original ambition

Record `report_year_gap = first_available_report_year − commitment_year`. It measures reporting-year separation, not elapsed months or equal exposure. Record original and follow-up reporting-period end dates and publication dates independently, with sources and explicit unknown values. Calculate publication-to-publication elapsed time only when both dates are known, naming those endpoints; do not substitute 1 January or an assumed publication date. Show reporting-year gaps even when exact dates are absent.

Before evaluating the later report, extract original-language quotations for: promised AI function/object; destination (study, pilot/test, deployment, other or unspecified); initial maturity; promised organisational/geographical/function scale; specificity components; deadline and its precision. Distinguish unspecified from source-unavailable. Do not infer ambition or a deadline from later achievements. Resolve relative deadlines only from an explicit reference date; otherwise retain the wording and uncertainty. Use a prespecified reference date and compatible date precision to classify a deadline as elapsed/not yet elapsed/indeterminate; record which reference date was used. Status and timeliness are separate: a future deadline is not failure, and fulfilment is not proof of timely fulfilment.

## 3. Outcome rubric: final F4 plus F5 AI-function requirement

Apply the frozen F4 achieved-state threshold and the F5 AI-function clarification to **only the selected report**, with original context available. The report may describe earlier events, but no evidence from reports after this selected report can support its classification. Record the source's event date separately when stated. Search the complete available report context, including source-language terms, project names and synonymous functions; dictionary hits alone are not an adequate negative search. Check extraction/OCR quality and relevant tables or images against the source where needed.

| Stored status | Required first-report evidence |
| --- | --- |
| `fulfilled` | The same promised AI function is reported achieved at the promised stage and scale. Completion or live operation of a promised study/pilot suffices when that was the undertaking. For a broad AI intention, at least one named implemented AI process consistent with its scope is required. |
| `partial` | Achieved-state evidence for that AI function exists, but only at a smaller scope, for some promised functions, or as a live pilot when deployment was promised. |
| `not_fulfilled` | Identifiable continuation concerns only development, preparation, testing without a live pilot, renewed plans, postponement or cancellation. Describe this as ongoing/other non-achieved reporting; record cancellation separately. |
| `dropped` | After an adequate search of an accessible, readable report, no identifiable continuation of the promised AI component is located. This means no located continuation in this report, not abandonment or cancellation. |
| `not_a_promise` | The original context does not establish a company undertaking to implement AI. Treat as an eligibility adjudication and retain its audit trail. |

Deployment of a host platform, service or process is insufficient unless evidence connects its operating **AI function** to the original undertaking. If only the host continues, with no identifiable AI-component continuation after adequate search, use `dropped`; if the AI component remains a plan/work in progress, use `not_fulfilled`; if part of the AI function operates, assess `partial`. Never infer AI operation from a generic digitalisation claim or from later reports. Supply original/follow-up quotation pairs and page/line locators, plus a short identity, stage, scope and AI-function rationale for each judgement. These are promise-relative categories; compare like ambitions and retain a sensitivity excluding broad undertakings.

## 4. Missingness and denominators

Keep source/assessment status separate from the outcome: source not retrieved; unreadable/incomplete extraction; uncertain entity match; incomplete search/context; ambiguous evidence pending adjudication; or assessable. Use blank outcome values with explicit reason codes for unresolved observations. An inaccessible report never becomes `dropped`, `not_fulfilled` or zero. Record retrieval attempts and recovery limits. Maintain all eligible rows in the flow table and report missingness by country, cohort, report-year gap and sector.

Any complete-case proportion targets **retrieved and assessable commitments in the selected corpus**, not all national firms or all commitments. Report numerator, assessable denominator, eligible denominator, missing count and firm count. Do not silently discard missing cases or report an unconditional national point estimate. Optional extreme-case bounds must name their finite target population and assumptions, including fixed eligibility and binary full-confirmation coding; they do not repair purposive sampling or missing entities.

## 5. Blinding, two independent rounds and adjudication

Prepare a clean source-only packet for every eligible case: neutral ID, original context, the selected report and inventory metadata. Exclude existing F3/F4/F5 outcomes, historical audit decisions, model-selected decisive quotations, later-report evidence, preferred country findings and the hidden human-validation key. Country and language may be evident from sources; document unavoidable unblinding rather than claim country blindness. Keep any ID crosswalk and historical outcomes outside coder-accessible materials.

Two independent rounds must cover **all** eligible cases, including cases previously agreed by models. Each coder applies the same frozen rubric and records source adequacy, original ambition, entity/object linkage, outcome, quotations and uncertainties independently. Neither round sees the other round's outputs or adjudications. Save immutable round outputs before comparison. Two passes by the same informed agent are not independent rounds. If model-based rounds are commissioned, disclose models/prompts and their limitations; they do not constitute independent human validation. Human validation requires independent, language-competent people and a documented completed exercise.

After both rounds close, calculate pre-adjudication agreement and confusion tables with explicit denominators, separately for assessability, linkage/eligibility and substantive outcomes; show country/language breakdowns where supported. Adjudicate every disagreement from sources, preserve both original judgements and reasons for the resolution, and leave unresolved cases missing. A rule amendment requires versioning and reassessment of all affected cases, not only disagreements. Historical outcome comparison and use of the hidden validation key remain outside this protocol's present drafting task and must follow the separate human-validation procedure.
