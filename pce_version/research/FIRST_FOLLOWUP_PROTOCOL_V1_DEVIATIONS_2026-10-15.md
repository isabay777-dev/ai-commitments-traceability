# Documented deviations and remaining limits — provisional first-report exercise v1.1

15 October 2026. This addendum preserves the frozen v1 outcome rule, selected-report inventory and original model-round files. It is not a retrospective claim of preregistration or author verification. The exercise is a provisional model-based comparison, not a completed human-validation study or full fulfilment of every measurement objective in v1.

1. **Independent linkage agreement unavailable.** The two rounds recorded narrative entity/year and object-linkage reasoning but did not both collect a common categorical linkage decision. Source-adequacy, eligibility and conditional substantive-status agreement can be calculated; a separate numerical entity/object-linkage agreement cannot. Post-hoc adjudicator judgements are not substituted for an independent pre-adjudication measure. The full narrative/source audit remains available.
2. **Timing measurement incomplete.** Original timing/deadline wording is retained verbatim. Publication dates and precise original/follow-up period-end dates were not uniformly verified. Calendar deadline precision and elapsed/not-yet-elapsed classification were not completed across all cases. `unknown` is retained; there is no inferred elapsed-month exposure, overdue label, on-time fulfilment measure or timeliness comparison. The tabulation of recorded timing wording is not called deadline completeness.
3. **Scope measurement is narrative plus broadness.** Promised AI function, stage and scope are adjudicated from source quotations. Stage and broadness/uncertain undertaking support are tabulated. Detailed organisational/geographical/functional scope and initial maturity remain in narrative/quote records rather than a uniform additional scale. Excluding broad undertakings does not fully harmonise ambition.
4. **Source-period inconsistency retained as missing.** When the visible report and selected inventory period conflict, no later report is substituted and no calendar year is silently corrected. Source-specific evidence and missingness reasons are retained. Availability of a raw/text file is not certification of the correct period or assessability.
5. **Exploratory support analysis refined after audit.** Exact years replace proposed pooled 2021–22 cohorts; common cells require both countries within sector×commitment year×report-year gap. No all-sample adjusted country coefficient is described as like-for-like. One-year-gap and common-cell results have separate denominators and exploratory interpretations. See `FIRST_REPORT_ANALYSIS_DECISIONS_2026-10-15.md`.

These limits must remain visible in the manuscript and supplement. The new first-report comparison can describe assessable cases in this selected corpus and display missingness/support; it cannot establish national implementation, institutional effects, coding accuracy or equal exposure. The 23 unexplained initial omissions and independent language-competent human validation remain separate unresolved work.
