# Specificity rule (clarified) — fixed 2026-10-11 before adjudication
Unit: the wording of one corporate commitment about the company's OWN AI. Later reports are not used.
spec2 (specific) = a concrete AI process/function/product/area is named AND at least one identifying or evaluable detail is stated:
 - a CURRENT achieved stage reported in the same fragment (a pilot/test already running or a solution already implemented that is to be extended); a future intention to "implement/introduce/develop" is NOT a stage;
 - a deadline or time frame; - a quantitative target or result; - an investment amount;
 - a named AI solution/product/platform/model; - a named external partner/vendor/institution.
spec1 (partly specific) = otherwise (including a named process with no such detail, or details without a named AI process).
