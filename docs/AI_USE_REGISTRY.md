# Supplementary file S2. Registry of AI use

This registry records research and manuscript-preparation stages, including stages run without a language model. It is based on the stage registry and its machine-readable companion as inspected on 9 October 2026, with subsequent decisions recorded on that date. A row describes a stage, not every parallel session. Unless otherwise indicated, the time shown is the output file's modification time in CEST (UTC+2), **not** a verified time of creation, execution, or human review; copying or changing a file may alter it. `NA` means that the supporting artifact was not found. Codex CLI version 0.155.1 was observed at inspection and in the available run headers; the CLI version does not identify an exact model snapshot. A model is not attributed to Python or R script execution merely because AI may have assisted with writing code. Prompt filenames below refer to the replication package's `prompts/` folder. Other filenames identify artifacts by basename only.

## Human oversight and interpretation

The earlier sampled verification covered 51/51 cases (40 randomly selected and stratified cases plus 11 difficult cases); all 51 model verdicts were accepted. This check preceded F3, was **not blind** because the reviewers saw the model verdicts, and did not cover the other 145 F2 records. The source workbook and decision record conflict over attribution of some reviews; this registry therefore does not assign individual reviewers to subsets or claim verified review dates. Approval of the 58 adjudications is a separate later check.

The decision record also documents human choices about the research focus, research questions, journal, and F2 then F3 releases. These choices do not establish manual checking of every fragment, model code, extracted variable, or manuscript revision. The available logs often identify model and reasoning effort; where a prompt, exact model, execution date, or human check is unsupported, the registry says `NA` rather than inferring one. A replication package was assembled on 9 October 2026; the decision record describes publication and DOI assignment as pending at that point. The same record states declarations of no funding, no conflicts of interest, and no ethics approval requirement for public documents; those declarations are not AI-processing stages.

The F4 revision (rows 27–34) added a further human step: an author reviewed and approved all 12 outcome changes from the harmonisation review. The 172 unchanged F4 outcomes rest on model agreement and the harmonisation review, not on independent human recoding.
